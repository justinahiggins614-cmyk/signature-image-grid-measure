1Load media

Choose any image from your device, or a video — play the video, pause on the frame you want, and hit Capture video frame. Nothing uploads anywhere; everything happens in your browser. The sample grid lets you practice with no file.

2Grid it

Pick Squares, Thirds, Golden ratio or Diagonals. Squares take a size slider. The grid sits on top of your image so every mark you make can be read against it.

3Measure it

Ruler — drag a line, read its length. Protractor — click three points; the second click is the corner, and you get the angle in degrees. Area — drag a rectangle, read its area. Color — click any pixel for its exact hex and RGB.

4Calibrate to the real world

Pick the Calibrate tool and drag along something in the image whose real size you know (a ruler, a doorway, a coin). Enter its real length and unit. From then on every reading is given in real units — millimetres to miles. Rotate the image the full 360° with the slider; your marks rotate with it.

5Recreate it

Build recreation report writes down everything: the image size, grid, rotation, scale and every measurement with its exact points. Download it as JSON, copy it, or download the marked image as PNG. Hand the report to anyone and they can reproduce every number exactly — that is what "recreatable" means here.

6Math tools & archive

The Math Tools tab holds Signature versions of the deterministic tools — unit converter, triangle solver, polygon solver, golden ratio, aspect ratio, circle math — all in standard math, same answers every time. The Measure Archive is the reference library: exact diagrams with every side, angle, area and perimeter worked out, marching to 1,000,000 records.
